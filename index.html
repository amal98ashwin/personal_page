Profile

Research that travels
from signal to clinic.

I am a translational neuroscience researcher working across computational and biological sciences. My work brings together electroencephalogram, electrogastrography, clinical measures, machine learning, and interactive technologies to study brain–body function and support clinical decision-making.

My experience spans IIT Kanpur, IIT Madras, TCS Research, and clinical collaborators. I enjoy building the full research pathway: shaping a question with clinicians, designing a study, collecting and analysing data, developing computational tools, and interpreting outcomes in a clinical context.

As a co-founder at Infinaid Technologies, I also work at the boundary between research, product thinking, and real-world neurotech and mental health support.

Clinical research leadership

From protocol to clinical evidence.

I have led two clinical trials from end to end, working to evaluate outcomes in depression care and compare treatment strategies.

01 / DEPRESSION CARE

Clinical validation of a treatment-outcome tool

Led an end-to-end clinical study to clinically validate a tool for tracking treatment outcomes in depression. The broader research programme explores multimodal measures—including EEG, electrogastrography, demographics, and questionnaires—to identify early response patterns.

Study leadership · clinical validation · multimodal neurophysiology
02 / TREATMENT COMPARISON

rTMS efficacy in first-episode depression

Led an end-to-end clinical trial comparing the efficacy of repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) as a treatment strategy for patients experiencing a first episode of depression.

Trial leadership · treatment evaluation · depression research
